## Gray Shifts

**Gray Shifts** is an independent single-player horror game currently in development by a small, focused team led by **Bardiya Shokri**. Built around atmosphere, tension and psychological horror, Gray Shifts is designed as a tightly crafted experience in which restraint matters as much as intensity.

This repository is the official public reference for the Gray Shifts game project.

## Project Overview

Gray Shifts is an indie horror game that explores atmosphere, tension and psychological horror through a deliberately designed single-player experience.

To preserve the experience for players, the project keeps its public information intentionally limited. Story, characters and gameplay details are not part of this page and will be revealed only through official announcements, when the team decides the time is right.

---

## Creative Direction

The creative direction of Gray Shifts is guided by a few core principles:

- **Atmosphere first.** Mood, pacing and presentation are treated as central design elements.
- **Tension over spectacle.** The project values quiet, controlled unease.
- **Focused scope.** A single-player experience shaped with intention rather than excess.
- **Discovery.** Players should experience Gray Shifts first-hand, not through a summary.

## Development Philosophy

Gray Shifts is being built with a simple approach: keep the scope clear, protect the experience and refine the details. As an independent Gray Shifts indie game, the project prioritizes a coherent identity and careful execution over scale.
